# Interface evidence gate

- The primary user journey works from entry to completion.
- Information hierarchy matches the task rather than a generic landing-page template.
- Tokens and component geometry remain coherent across the whole surface.
- Text and controls meet applicable WCAG contrast and keyboard requirements.
- Responsive behavior is explicit for every multi-column or gesture-heavy section.
- Loading, empty, error, disabled, focus, hover/press, and reduced-motion states exist where relevant.
- Motion has a stated communication purpose and does not block interaction.
- Existing routes, analytics, copy voice, legal content, and accessible behavior were not silently broken during redesign.
- Screenshots or interaction evidence cover the most important narrow and wide states.
