# Blinded qualitative gate

Use this only after functional and accessibility checks pass.

Give the evaluator the brief, relevant `DESIGN.md`, and screenshots or rendered output. Do not reveal the intended score or implementation rationale. Ask it to identify concrete evidence for:

1. fit to audience and product intent;
2. hierarchy and visual coherence;
3. presence of generic AI-template patterns;
4. clarity of primary action;
5. consistency between narrow and wide layouts.

Require cited visual observations, not adjectives alone. Treat the evaluation as diagnostic evidence, not an authority that overrides user intent or measurable failures.
